## Response (verbatim)

````
The strongest publicly documented results are **vendor case studies and one university comparison—not independent, audited proof of improved bid win rates**. Among the named vendors, the available evidence here is detailed mainly for Togal.AI; it does not establish comparable customer metrics for Beam AI, Kreo, Attentive.ai, Bluebeam Max, STACK, Downtobid, or Trunk Tools.

| Vendor | Documented result and customer | What the evidence supports |
|---|---|---|
| **Togal.AI** | A University of Kansas study comparing Togal with On-Screen Takeoff reported roughly **70–76% less takeoff time** across its test cases. After manual adjustments, most measured quantities were within **5%** of the comparison results; the study also notes classification and drawing-interpretation errors.[4][6] | A limited software comparison on selected plans—not proof of equivalent accuracy across live bids or trades. |
| **Togal.AI** | **Total Flooring Contractors** reported completing a 30-story high-rise takeoff in **48 hours instead of its usual two weeks**, catching drawing errors before bid submission, and winning the contract; Togal says the customer later won another project with the same construction company.[1][5][8] | A customer-reported turnaround and wins; no controlled comparison showing Togal caused the wins. |
| **Togal.AI** | **Coastal Construction** reports saving an average of **14.5 hours per plan set** and nearly **14,000 hours** in its first year; the vendor’s case-study page also lists **50–65% faster preconstruction** and estimated savings of **about $1 million in the first year**.[2][5] | Customer/vendor-reported productivity and savings, not independently audited financial results. |
| **Togal.AI** | The vendor reports **NC Painting** increased its bid rate by **215% in under two months** after switching from Bluebeam.[8] | “Bid rate” is reported, but the cited material does not define the denominator, starting volume, or whether the increase means bids submitted or bids won. |
| **Togal.AI** | **Select Painting** is named in Togal’s case-study list as winning more work, but the material returned here gives no quantified win-rate result.[2] | A qualitative vendor claim, not a measured win-rate estimate. |

### How to interpret the numbers

- **Time savings are the best-supported metric**, especially for Togal: the university comparison supplies a defined task comparison, while contractor case studies report operational savings.[4][6][1][5]
- **Accuracy evidence is narrower.** The university study compares outputs with On-Screen Takeoff on selected plans and says most adjusted quantities were within 5%; that is not the same as verified bid or installed-quantity accuracy across a contractor’s project portfolio.[4][6]
- **Bid volume and win rate are poorly documented in the material available.** Togal’s reported 215% increase concerns bid rate, while the Total Flooring account describes individual wins; neither provides a controlled or standardized win-rate measure.[8][1]
- **For Beam AI, Kreo, Attentive.ai, Bluebeam Max, STACK, Downtobid, and Trunk Tools**, no customer-specific quantified result surfaced in the available sources here. That should be read as **no substantiated figure in this evidence set**, not as evidence that those products have no results.

**Bottom line:** Publicly visible evidence supports faster takeoff workflows and suggests potential gains in estimator capacity. Claims about accuracy, bids submitted per estimator, and especially bid win rate are less standardized and should be checked against named customer references, baselines, and the exact metric definition before being treated as comparable results.
````
