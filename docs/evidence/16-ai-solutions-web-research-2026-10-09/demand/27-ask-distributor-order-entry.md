## Response

````markdown
**Short answer:** Publicly available evidence is thin and vendor-skewed. Conexiom publishes touchless and operational-impact claims; Endeavor publishes accuracy and time-saved claims, but few named-customer metrics are available in the results reviewed. Comparable, independently verified figures—and public contract pricing—are generally missing.

## Reported automation and accuracy

- **Conexiom:** Its materials claim roughly **85–87% touchless processing**, **over 95% accuracy**, and **50% fewer order errors**. Another vendor page reports **30% faster fulfillment**. These are company-reported figures; the pages do not specify a consistent measurement method or customer-level baseline.[2][6]
- **Endeavor:** It claims **90%+ accuracy** and **95%+ fewer human corrections** on a chemical-distributor customer page. Separately, its product page says order entry time can fall by **up to 90%**. These are not the same metric as touchless rate, and the available material does not establish that 90%+ accuracy means 90% of orders pass without review.[7][10]
- **Endeavor’s “100% accuracy” language** is framed as a product claim after catalog setup, not as a reported independent production result; it also describes orders being presented for review.[1]
- **Rossum, Hyperscience, Tacto, and Doss:** The available search results did not provide comparable, verifiable sales-order-specific touchless or accuracy figures for these vendors. General document-processing accuracy claims should not be treated as sales-order straight-through-processing rates.

## Named-customer outcomes

The results available here do **not** substantiate named customers’ before-and-after figures for **orders per rep, order-entry error rates, or order cycle time**. Conexiom’s material gives platform-level claims such as **2× CSR productivity**, but not a named customer’s measured orders-per-rep result.[6] Endeavor’s customer page reports **90%+ accuracy** and **95%+ fewer corrections** for a chemical distributor, but the result shown does not identify the customer or provide baseline volumes or cycle-time data.[7]

Treat these as leads for diligence, not apples-to-apples benchmarks. Ask vendors for customer references in your industry and for definitions of “touchless,” the measurement period, order volume and line complexity, exception rate, and the baseline used.

## What it costs

The reviewed results do **not** establish credible enterprise contract prices for these products. Endeavor says pricing is based on automated order-entry volume and offers pilots, but provides no rate card.[1] Conexiom appears to sell by quote; third-party directory listings showing token prices such as **$1 per user per year** are not a reliable proxy for the cost of an enterprise deployment.[3][5]

Request a quote that separates implementation and ERP integration, subscription or transaction charges, minimum annual volume, exception handling, and ongoing model/configuration support. For comparison, calculate total annual cost per **successfully processed order**, not just cost per document or license.
````
