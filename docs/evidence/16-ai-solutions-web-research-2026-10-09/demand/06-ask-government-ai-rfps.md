# 06 — perplexity_ask — government AI RFPs and contracts

- **Tool:** `mcp__perplexity__perplexity_ask` (search_context_size: high)
- **Query:** "What AI solutions are US federal, state and local governments issuing RFPs and contracts for in 2025-2026? Give concrete examples by problem (e.g. benefits eligibility and fraud, call center, permit review, 311, document processing, traffic, child welfare risk, procurement), agency, contract value, and vendor where known."
- **Timestamp (UTC):** 2026-10-09T20:21:56Z

## Response

````markdown
The strongest documented pattern in 2025–26 is procurement of **workflow automation and AI-enabled case or service systems**, not necessarily fully autonomous decision-making. Publicly disclosed prices are uneven; several advertised “AI contract” examples are procurements for broader IT systems, and should not be treated as AI awards.

| Problem | Agency and procurement | Value / vendor | What the evidence supports |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Child-welfare case management and information systems** | HHS Administration for Children and Families task for child-welfare information-systems support | **$69.77 million** total contract value; **ICF**; awarded in 2025, with work through 2030[1] | This is a substantial child-welfare IT contract, but the available notice does **not** establish that it is an AI risk-scoring or predictive child-welfare contract. |
| **Child-welfare system modernization** | Virginia Department of Social Services RFP for a Comprehensive Child Welfare Information System (CCWIS) | Estimated **$5–20 million**; vendor and award value not yet known in the notice[3] | The RFP concerns replacing and modernizing the case system; the posted estimate is explicitly an estimate, not an awarded price. |
| **Child-welfare system modernization** | North Dakota Department of Human Services RFP for a cloud-based CCWIS | Vendor and price not stated; a third-party listing gives a **$5–20 million estimate** and projected award date in 2026[9] | This is a child-welfare software procurement, not evidence of an AI-based risk prediction tool. |
| **Contact center / citizen service** | GSA Public Experience Contact Center Services BPA | Value and vendor not stated in the available GSA use-case listing[6] | The stated goal is to use AI to reduce calls, emails, and chats across a program handling more than 2 million inquiries annually. |
| **Benefits fraud / identity verification** | GSA Login.gov use case for remote identity proofing | Value and vendor not stated[6] | The listed AI use is detecting fraud, spoofing, or deepfakes in remote identity verification—not determining benefits eligibility. |
| **Procurement and contract administration** | GSA acquisition-workflow and electronic contract-file use cases | Value and vendor not stated[6] | GSA describes AI to automate acquisition data entry and help contracting officers review and categorize files. These are listed as pre-deployment/development or acquisition use cases, not disclosed awards. |
| **Public records, redaction, transcription, and video analytics** | TXShare cooperative purchasing award to VIDIZMO | Contract value not stated; **VIDIZMO**[7] | Participating public entities can procure AI-enabled redaction, transcription/translation, video analytics, evidence management, and search through the cooperative vehicle. This is access to a competitively sourced contract, rather than a single agency’s priced RFP. |

**What is not established in these results:** a named 2025–26 award with disclosed price and vendor specifically for automated benefits eligibility decisions, municipal permit review, 311 automation, traffic management, or child-welfare risk scoring. For child welfare, the cited procurements are for broader information systems; calling them predictive-AI contracts would overstate the evidence.

A practical distinction when tracking this market: **“AI use case,” “AI-accessible purchasing vehicle,” “IT modernization RFP,” and “awarded AI contract” are different things.** GSA’s listed uses include pre-deployment activities, while the VIDIZMO item is a cooperative purchasing award and the child-welfare examples are systems procurements.[6][7][3]
````
