Machine learning can help CPG and distribution finance teams distinguish legitimate trade spend from invalid short-pays, validate claims against promotions, invoices, pricing and delivery evidence, prioritize recoverable deductions, and match remittances to open invoices. The strongest named-customer metrics in the available sources are from HighRadius; they do not provide comparable, verified outcome data here for iNymbus, Vistex, Billtrust, Esker, or Cash Flow Analytics.

| Customer / vendor | Reported result | What it covers |
|---|---|---|
| **Danone North America / HighRadius** | Recovered **$25.5 million annually** in invalid deductions; reduced days deduction outstanding (DDO) by **25 days**; improved analyst productivity by **75%**. | Danone managed more than $400 million in deductions and 1.1 million claims a year, including claims involving Walmart and Kroger. HighRadius reports ML-based validity scoring, automated claim documentation, and matching against promotion and sales data.[2] |
| **Hershey’s / HighRadius** | **70% fewer Kroger deductions requiring manual effort** and a **40% reduction** in concentrated open-deduction dollar value. | The case describes AI-assisted deduction research and automated resolution for matched claims.[3] |
| **Clorox / HighRadius** | Automated cash application rose to **80%**, from roughly **50–55%**; payment-posting time fell **64%**. | The reported workflow extracts remittance data from emails, EDI and portals, then matches payments to invoices and codes deductions.[6] |

These cases illustrate two related but distinct workflows: **deduction management**—claim intake, validity prediction, evidence gathering, dispute and recovery—and **cash application**—remittance extraction, invoice matching and exception handling. The cited sources report recovery, DDO and automation results, but do **not** give a comparable customer-specific auto-match rate for every named vendor.

For vendor evaluation, ask each provider for customer references in your channel and ERP, with before-and-after figures for dollars recovered, DDO, recovery rate, and cash-application auto-match rate. Also clarify the denominator for “auto-match” (payment, remittance, or invoice line) and whether the result is independently verified.
