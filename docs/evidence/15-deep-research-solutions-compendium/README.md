## What this is

The **external Deep Research answer** to the solutions prompt
([`../../prompts/solutions-deep-research-prompt.md`](../../prompts/solutions-deep-research-prompt.md)).
Per its pasted summary: **43 solution dossiers** across all 10 families (A–J) on the
18-field template, anchored on the churn prediction & retention program (point-in-time
snapshots, temporal validation, label windows, save-desk wiring, retention holdouts),
with solution-specific roles/cadences, automation frontiers, domain proof loops, latency
regimes, and domain governance (ECOA adverse action, SR 11-7, OT safety, EU AI Act);
plus the **seven cross-cutting syntheses** (anatomy with a 43-row mapping,
correctness-adaptation catalog, methodology → solutions fan-out, action-integration
patterns, measurement-loop patterns, why-solutions-fail grounded in documented cases —
Zillow Offers, Knight Capital, Watson for Oncology, Amazon recruiting, Epic sepsis, the
Dutch benefits scandal, Apple Card — and a maturity ranking).

**Source-stated process note (from the summary, preserved):** the first two drafts had
templated fields repeated across dossiers; the research agent was sent back twice with
verified acceptance criteria before this version passed, with every field unique per
solution.

**Counterpart:** Claude's internal answer to the same prompt is
[`../12-claude-solutions-compendium/`](../12-claude-solutions-compendium/)
(34 dossiers) — the two were produced independently for comparison.
