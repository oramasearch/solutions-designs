The clearest public, quantified examples are from **Legion**, but they mainly show labor forecasting, scheduling, and overtime control—not machine-learning detection of buddy punching or payroll fraud. Public evidence for named vendors tying anomaly detection or hourly-hire prediction to measured outcomes is sparse in the available results.

- **Legion—retail overtime:** A Forrester study of four retailer clients reported a **10% reduction in overtime pay** in its modeled organization; one California retailer separately reported a **10% reduction in overtime** after using forecasting and compliance flags to avoid schedules crossing overtime thresholds.[2][8][1]
- **Legion—retailer savings:** A national discount retailer reported **nearly $10 million in savings**, a **500-basis-point improvement in workload-scheduling efficiency**, and forecasts **over 60% more accurate** than its previous methods. The case describes visibility into employees’ hours as a way to avoid triggering overtime when moving staff between stores.[6]
- **Legion—recent composite analysis:** A Forrester study reports modeled benefits of **$1.6 million in reduced overtime** and a **20% decrease in overtime hours** versus pre-adoption trends. These are composite-model estimates, not a single employer’s audited savings.[10]
- **Legion—turnover:** The earlier Forrester analysis estimated a **5% reduction in employee turnover** for its composite organization; a newer study modeled a **10% reduction in voluntary turnover**. These results concern overall turnover, not specifically 90-day retention or a hiring-quality score.[2][7]
- **Legion—restaurant operations:** A fast-casual chain reported employees averaged **10% more hours per week** after shift swaps and open-shift pickup became easier, while the company reduced overtime spend; the case does not quantify the overtime reduction in dollars.[3]

**What this supports:** A credible shortlist for labor-cost control is Legion, especially where the goal is demand forecasting, schedule optimization, overtime visibility, and compliance alerts. The cited examples do **not** establish measured results for payroll-error rates, buddy-punching detection, or early-tenure retention/quality-of-hire prediction from UKG, Workstream, Harri, Fountain, or Arena Analytics. Those should be treated as separate claims requiring vendor-specific case studies and clear outcome definitions.
