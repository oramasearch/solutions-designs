**Arena Analytics has the clearest public, employer-named evidence.** The results below are the specific measured early-retention or turnover figures I could substantiate; the evidence for the other platforms is weaker or does not report a quantified retention outcome.

- **Sunrise Senior Living** — Arena’s predictive analytics reportedly improved **90-day employee retention by 27%** and **one-year retention by 14%** across Sunrise’s 265 U.S. communities. The source does not clarify whether the 27% is a relative increase or percentage-point change.[1]
- **Mount Sinai Health System** — Arena reported **90-day turnover reduced by 77.6%**, with 180-day turnover down 34.3% and 360-day turnover down 25.7% after two years. This is a named healthcare employer, but the source describes the health system broadly rather than specifying hourly aide roles.[3]
- **Unnamed nationwide senior-living provider** — Arena’s pilot found turnover among recommended applicants was **22% lower at 90 days** and 28% lower at 60 days than among non-recommended applicants. The employer is not named, so it does not meet the named-employer criterion.[2]
