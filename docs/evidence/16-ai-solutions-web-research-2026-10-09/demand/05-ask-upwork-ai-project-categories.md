The strongest demand is for **applied AI that solves a defined business workflow**—integrating models with company data, software, and operations—rather than building a new foundation model. Upwork’s 2025 report found rapid growth in generative-AI modeling and AI data annotation; a report on 2025 marketplace hiring later reported especially strong growth in AI video, AI integration, annotation, and chatbot development.[1][3][7]

## Upwork’s fastest-growing AI skills

Upwork’s 2025 skills report analyzed marketplace data from January through October 2024. It highlighted **AI data annotation and labeling, knowledge representation, and generative AI modeling** among the fastest-growing AI-related skills; the report said generative-AI modeling and annotation grew by as much as 220% year over year. It also reported that generative-AI modeling freelancers could earn up to 22% more hourly than traditional AI/ML roles.[1][3]

A separate report covering U.S. marketplace hiring during 2025 said AI skills grew 109% year over year on average. It reported growth of **329% for AI video generation and editing, 178% for AI integration, 154% for data annotation and labeling, and 71% for chatbot development**.[7] These figures come from different reporting periods and should not be treated as directly comparable.

## Most in-demand project categories and the problems behind them

- **AI agents and workflow automation:** Build agents that qualify leads, answer questions, book appointments, summarize or route messages, and update CRMs. Jobs often combine an LLM with tool calling, memory, APIs, and business rules.[4][5]
- **Chatbots and RAG:** Create website, messaging, or internal assistants grounded in company documents or databases. Common requirements include retrieval-augmented generation (RAG), vector stores, and integrations with booking systems or CRMs.[4][8]
- **Voice agents:** Handle inbound calls or outbound follow-ups, collect caller details, qualify leads, book appointments, and log outcomes in a CRM. Listings reference voice platforms connected to n8n or Make and other business tools.[6][10]
- **n8n, Make, and API automation:** Replace repetitive manual work by connecting email, messaging apps, forms, CRMs, calendars, databases, and SaaS tools. Typical tasks include lead capture, document processing, follow-up sequences, order handling, and internal reporting.[2][5]
- **Data annotation and evaluation:** Label or review data used to train and assess AI systems. Upwork identifies annotation and labeling as a fast-growing skill area.[1][3]
- **Forecasting and data science:** Build predictive models or analytics for business planning and decision support. The available results support broader demand for data science and analytics, but do not provide a specific 2025–26 growth figure or standard project budget for forecasting.[7]
- **Generative AI and model customization:** Develop or adapt models for a particular use case, including fine-tuning. These projects can require more specialized ML skills than workflow integrations.[1][8]
- **AI-generated creative work:** Produce and refine AI-generated video or images for marketing, training, and other content needs. AI video generation and editing had the largest reported growth in the 2025 report.[7]

## Rates and project budgets

Upwork’s AI category page lists **$35–$60 per hour** as the median hourly range for artificial intelligence engineers.[8] Its 2025 report says generative-AI modeling can command a premium of up to 22% over traditional AI/ML hourly roles, but the retrieved sources do not give a universal rate card for each project type.[1]

Visible job examples show how widely fixed budgets vary: a car-wash booking chatbot was listed at **$300**, an AI workflow project at **$100**, and a model fine-tuning challenge at **$2,000**.[8] These are individual listings, not reliable market averages; project scope, integrations, deployment, testing, and ongoing support can change the price substantially.

**Practical takeaway:** For marketplace positioning, a strong portfolio should demonstrate an end-to-end business outcome—such as a RAG support assistant connected to a CRM, or a voice agent that books appointments and triggers follow-up—not just familiarity with a model or automation tool. Upwork listings emphasize integrations, reliability, testing, and handoff alongside the AI component.[4][5][6]
