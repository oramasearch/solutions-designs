- **Query:** "What productized AI packages do boutique AI agencies and AI automation consultancies sell to mid-market and small businesses in 2025-2026? List specific named offerings (e.g. AI receptionist build, lead reactivation, AI-powered quoting, document processing, custom GPT knowledge base, sales call analysis), their typical fixed price or retainer ranges, and which industries buy them most."
- **Timestamp (UTC):** 2026-10-09T20:21:56Z

## Response

````markdown
There is **no standard price list**: packages range from a few thousand dollars for a focused SMB workflow to tens of thousands for integrated, production-grade AI systems. The ranges below are indicative; scope, integrations, call volume, and ongoing support can shift them substantially.[1][2][7][10]

| Named package | Typical price seen | Industries that commonly buy it |
|---|---:|---|
| **AI receptionist / voice agent** — answers calls, qualifies callers, books appointments, logs to CRM, escalates exceptions | **$2,000–$15,000 setup**, often **$200–$1,500/month**; some smaller offers start around **$990/month**[6][9][10] | Home services and trades, clinics and other appointment-based businesses, real estate, multi-location service firms[1][6][9] |
| **Lead reactivation / database revival** — SMS, email, or voice outreach to dormant leads and past customers | Roughly **$750–$2,500/month** for managed tiers; packaged setup-plus-retainer offers also appear around **$1,500 setup + $497/month**[8][9] | Home services, clinics, real estate, and other businesses with sizable customer or inquiry lists[1][8][9] |
| **Lead follow-up, qualification, and booking automation** — responds to new inquiries, nurtures prospects, routes qualified leads, and books appointments | Typically part of **$3,000–$25,000** workflow builds, with **$150–$800/month** maintenance; simpler bundles can be less[1][10] | Home services, real estate, professional services, agencies, and appointment-driven businesses[1][5][10] |
| **AI quoting / estimate workflow** — collects job details, drafts or automates estimates, and hands off exceptions for review | Often sold within **$5,000–$15,000** home-services automation builds; published general workflow ranges extend to **$25,000** depending on integrations[1][10] | HVAC, plumbing, roofing, landscaping, and other field-service businesses[1] |
| **Document processing / invoice and PDF automation** — extracts data from invoices, forms, contracts, or records and routes it into business systems | Prototypes from about **$2,000**; production builds from **$5,000**; complex single-use-case deployments may be **$30,000–$60,000**[2][7] | Finance and back-office teams, professional services, healthcare, and businesses with high document volumes[2][7] |
| **Custom GPT / internal knowledge assistant** — answers staff questions from company documents and systems, often with access controls and guardrails | A basic prototype or scoped build can start near **$2,000–$5,000**; a production single-use-case deployment may run **$30,000–$60,000**[2][7] | Professional services, agencies, and document-heavy organizations; legal and healthcare are examples of document-intensive use cases cited by providers[2] |
| **Sales-call analysis / sales intelligence** — summarizes calls, extracts objections and action items, and supports coaching or CRM updates | The available pricing results don’t establish a reliable boutique-agency fixed-price range; adjacent AI sales-agent subscriptions range from **$500–$6,000/month per agent**, but that is not the same as a call-analysis implementation[4] | Sales-led teams, especially agencies and mid-market businesses, though the results don’t quantify industry demand specifically[4] |
| **E-commerce operations automation** — order, fulfillment, inventory, customer-support triage, or review workflows | Roughly **$4,000–$10,000 per build**, with retainers around **$800–$2,000/month** in one agency pricing guide[1] | Shopify merchants and other e-commerce businesses[1] |
| **Automation audit / sprint** — maps workflows, identifies opportunities, or delivers a tested prototype and roadmap | Audit around **$500–$2,000**; a two-week prototype sprint from **$2,000**; ongoing support commonly **$500–$3,000/month**[1][7] | SMBs and midsize teams across sectors; generally an entry offer leading to implementation work[1][7] |

A practical pattern is **fixed-fee discovery or prototype → fixed-scope implementation → monthly maintenance and iteration**. The cited guide places common SMB builds around **$3,000–$15,000** and retainers around **$500–$3,000/month**, while more complex, integrated deployments can cost much more.[1][2] Industry-specific demand is clearest in home services, e-commerce, and real estate; document processing and knowledge assistants are broader cross-industry offers rather than products tied to one dominant vertical.[1][2]
````
