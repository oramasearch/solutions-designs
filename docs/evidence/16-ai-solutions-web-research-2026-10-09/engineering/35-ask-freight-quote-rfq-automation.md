## Response (verbatim)

````
Documented outcomes exist, but the evidence is uneven: public numbers are mostly vendor-reported case studies or marketing claims, not independently audited, controlled comparisons. Of the requested freight vendors, the available results here provide a concrete customer metric for Greenscreens.ai and a response-time claim for Vooma; they do not substantiate comparable outcome metrics for Parade, HappyRobot, FleetOps, or Loadsmart.

### Freight brokers and 3PLs

- **Greenscreens.ai — rate prediction and pricing:** Southland reported a **21% increase in load count in its first month** after adopting Greenscreens. It used predicted rates and historical data to identify lanes, guide pricing, and monitor margins; the account does **not** report a quantified margin-per-load or win-rate improvement.[6]
- **Greenscreens.ai — pricing rationale:** The vendor describes real-time market buy-rate predictions and suggested sell-price guidance as tools to help balance win rate and margin, but the cited material gives no measured customer uplift for either metric.[7]
- **Vooma — email-to-quote:** A freight-tools roundup says WorldWide Logistics reduced **individual quote response time by 95%** using Vooma. That is a vendor case-study claim relayed by the roundup; no baseline time or change in quotes per rep, win rate, or margin is provided.[4]
- **Parade, HappyRobot, FleetOps, and Loadsmart:** No specific, verifiable customer outcome metrics for quote response time, quotes per rep, win rate, or margin per load appear in the available sources. This does not establish that the vendors lack such results—only that they are not supported by the sources available here.

### Supplier RFQs and quote comparison in procurement

- **Keelvar:** The company reports **$60 million in cost avoidance** for a global technology company that moved from annual to bi-monthly sourcing.[8][9] A separate vendor-oriented roundup says Keelvar reports **12–18% average savings** in competitive sourcing events, but that figure is not an independently validated benchmark.[1]
- **Fairmarkit:** A procurement roundup reports company-claimed **11% average savings**, **40% shorter sourcing cycle times**, and **10× more sourcing events per FTE**; another roundup gives different figures—**86% shorter intake-to-award cycle time** and **300% more events per buyer**.[2][5] These are vendor-reported aggregate claims, and the sources do not explain enough about methodology or overlap to reconcile the differing cycle-time figures.

**Bottom line:** The clearest named customer-specific freight result in these sources is Southland’s 21% load-count increase; Vooma has a reported 95% faster quote response. Procurement sources cite substantial savings and throughput gains for Keelvar and Fairmarkit, but mostly as vendor-reported claims. The available evidence does not support a reliable cross-vendor comparison on quotes per rep, win rate, or margin per load.
````
