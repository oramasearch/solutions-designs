**Named, quantified deployments span document review, contract analysis, legal billing and non-AML compliance.** The figures below are reported case-study outcomes, not standardized comparisons; where a result reflects a whole workflow rather than AI alone, the table says so.

| Application | Named customer | AI/ML deployment | Reported measured result |
|---|---|---|---|
| E-discovery: TAR / predictive coding | Foley & Lardner | RelativityOne active learning prioritized documents in a 117-million-document investigation. | Estimated **$10 million saved** and investigation completed **10× faster**. Relativity attributes both results to the platform *and* active-learning workflow together.[web:248] |
| E-discovery: TAR / predictive coding | McDonald Carano | Everlaw predictive coding followed search, date filtering and email threading. | The combined process reduced the original data volume requiring review by **96%**; the source does not quantify dollar savings.[web:237] |
| E-discovery: TAR / predictive coding | Fulbright & Jaworski | Predictive coding assisted an eight-reviewer team assessing **500,000 documents**. | The team manually reviewed **less than 8%** of the collection; the account reports thousands of review hours saved, without an exact dollar figure.[web:250] |
| Contract risk and obligation review | Freshfields Bruckhaus Deringer | Kira extracted potentially problematic provisions from multilingual distribution contracts for legal review and remediation. | In the documented healthcare-compliance project, the first **4,000 contracts** were reviewed in **under three months**; no percentage cost saving is given for that project.[web:231] |
| Contract risk and obligation review | Freshfields Bruckhaus Deringer | Kira-assisted contract review on a separately reported project. | **20–40% efficiency gains** reported; this should not be treated as the measured saving for the healthcare project above.[web:228] |
| Contract due diligence | Bird & Bird | Luminance analyzed employment-contract documents and surfaced potential anomalies and risks. | After a test phase, **two associates reviewed 100%** of documents relating to nearly **20,000 employees in three weeks**; previously **eight associates reviewed 10%**, relating to 1,900 employees, in three weeks.[web:3] |
| Contract review | LG Chem | Luminance reviewed contracts using a knowledge bank informed by previously agreed terms. | **At least 30% less review time per contract**.[web:395] |
| Contract review | FlexGen | Evisort AI supported contract-management and review workflows. | The customer case study reports **90% less contract-review time**; it does not publish the underlying timing baseline.[web:136] |
| Contract search and obligation visibility | Workday | Evisort searched and extracted information across Workday’s contract estate. | Found **two relevant contracts among 88,000 in under an hour**. Workday also reports approximately **3,500% ROI**, calculated against its *presumed outsourced-search costs*, rather than an independently verified baseline.[web:141] |
| Contract-template risk review | Orangetheory | Ironclad AI Assist helped compare and redline membership agreements against required terms. | Consolidated approximately **1,000 templates in three months**, versus a **six-month project goal**; its legal-operations lead said AI produced the needed result about **80% of the time**.[web:220] |
| Contract data extraction | Rippling | Ironclad Smart Import extracted information from legacy contracts. | Uploading legacy contracts became **40–50% faster**, with **2–3× more contract data** captured than before, according to its legal and compliance analyst.[web:214] |
| Legal invoice and outside-counsel spend review | Ocado Group | Brightflag AI-assisted invoice review applied billing guidelines, checked discounts and flagged violations. | **5% savings in year one**, against a **3.3% target**, plus **1,470 administrative hours saved**. The savings reflect the combined billing-control process.[web:363] |
| Legal invoice and outside-counsel spend review | Syngenta | Brightflag supported invoice review, rejection of noncompliant expenses and spend controls. | Recouped the equivalent of **10% of gross legal spend in H1 2021**; the case study does not isolate the share due solely to AI invoice review.[web:267] |
| Legal invoice and outside-counsel spend review | SMBC Americas Division | Brightflag AI-enabled review applied outside-counsel guidelines and negotiated discounts to invoices. | **$2.7 million saved in nine months**; separately, automated matter intake saved **over 300 labor-hours**.[web:296] |
| Legal invoice and outside-counsel spend review | AccuWeather | Brightflag automated billing review and helped identify discrepancies in outside-counsel charges. | An account featuring its legal leader reports **close to 30% savings in outside-counsel payments** and invoice-administration time falling from **more than 40 to about five hours per month**. It does not isolate an ML-only effect.[web:365] |
| Legal invoice and outside-counsel spend review | PNC Bank | Wolters Kluwer LegalVIEW BillAnalyzer replaced attorney-led invoice checks with AI-powered analysis and automated validation. | **Billing-guideline compliance increased 10% in the first month**; PNC says it met first-year savings expectations but gives **no customer-specific savings percentage**. Wolters Kluwer’s separate “up to 10% savings” figure is a product claim, not PNC’s result.[web:345] |
| Regulatory-obligation mapping | ING and Commonwealth Bank of Australia | Ascent used NLP and AI in a joint, regulator-overseen MiFID II/MiFIR pilot. | Ascent reports completing analysis in **2.5 minutes**, compared with approximately **1,800 hours** of manual obligation-mapping effort. The banks’ account confirms the pilot and reports hundreds of manual hours saved.[web:192][web:195] |
| Market-abuse / trade surveillance | Capital Markets Authority of Saudi Arabia | Nasdaq AI anomaly-detection tooling was tested in an **eight-month pilot**. | Identified **80% of pump-and-dump schemes in a historical sample set**. This is a back-tested detection result, **not** a reported live-market false-positive reduction.[web:162] |
| Communications and policy/conduct surveillance | Osaic | Theta Lake’s AI-enabled video, audio and chat supervision flagged potential policy, conduct and regulatory risks. | Reported **3,300 hours saved annually**, equivalent to **two full-time employees**. That figure covers the *entire* automated supervision workflow, including ingestion and archiving, not AI detection alone.[web:323] |
| Sanctions and customer screening | Banco do Brasil | Napier AI transaction screening incorporated sanctions lists and tailored screening configurations. | **More than 90% fewer false-positive hits**; the reported false-positive rate fell to **2%**.[web:178] |
| Sanctions and customer screening | IPT Africa | ComplyAdvantage automated ongoing screening against sanctions and watchlists, PEPs and adverse-media data. | **60% fewer false positives** across that combined customer-risk screening workflow; the source does not separate sanctions-only alerts.[web:182] |
