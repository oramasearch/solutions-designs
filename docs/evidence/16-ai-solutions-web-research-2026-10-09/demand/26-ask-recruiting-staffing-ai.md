## Response

````markdown
AI recruiting tools generally **recommend, rank, screen, contact, and schedule** candidates; they rarely make the final hiring decision. Public evidence is much stronger for high-volume enterprise deployments and vendor-reported productivity metrics than for independently verified placement gains or SMB pricing.

| Tool | Decisions and tasks it handles | Reported outcomes | SMB pricing signal |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Paradox (Olivia)** | Conversational intake and pre-screening, candidate FAQs, qualification questions, and interview scheduling by chat or text; it advances candidates through a workflow rather than making the final hire decision.[5] | Chipotle reported application completion rising from 50% to 85% and application-to-start time dropping from 12 days to 4 days.[9] A Workday deployment reported 23,000 hours saved over two years and interview confirmations cut from days to hours.[7] These are client/vendor case-study results, not general SMB benchmarks. | Usually custom/enterprise; one comparison estimates $1,000+/month, while another gives a higher estimate. Treat these as rough, unverified anchors, not quotes.[5][9] |
| **HireVue** | Structured video interviews and assessments help screen and compare applicants; the platform standardizes evaluation, but employers set criteria and make hiring decisions.[4][9] | The sources reviewed describe its enterprise positioning but provide no comparable, well-specified SMB outcome figure.[4][9] | Enterprise/custom pricing; a secondary estimate puts it at $35,000+/year, so it may be a poor fit for a small employer unless buying only a narrow module.[9] |

| **Tezi, Juicebox, Alex, Apriora** | The available results do not provide dependable, tool-specific detail on which hiring decisions each makes, validated outcome metrics, or SMB prices. | No comparable measured outcomes were found in the results reviewed. | Pricing not established by the available sources; request a quote and confirm implementation, usage, and seat fees. |

**What the numbers do—and don’t—say**

- **Time to fill:** The clearest tool-specific case here is Paradox/Chipotle’s 12-to-4-day application-to-start reduction; it is a single high-volume customer example, not an expected SMB result.[9]
- **Recruiter capacity:** Bullhorn’s cited estimates include up to 4.5 hours saved per recruiter weekly on candidate search and up to 17 hours across AI-enabled tasks, but these are survey estimates.[1]
- **Placements:** Bullhorn reports higher placements and fill rates, but those figures reflect vendor-reported benchmarks; they do not establish that AI alone caused the gains.[1][10]
- **Pricing:** Public anchors are most useful for budgeting Bullhorn seats. Paradox and HireVue are generally quote-led and appear enterprise-oriented; pricing claims for smaller tools should be verified directly before comparison.[5][9][10]

For an SMB buyer, assess the tool against one workflow and measure **time-to-first-qualified-candidate, interview completion, recruiter hours per filled role, and fill rate**. Ask vendors for the same figures from customers similar in hiring volume and role type, plus total cost including setup and integrations.
````
