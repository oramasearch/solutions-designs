**How to read the Buildcheck figures:** they are vendor-published case studies, and the customer organizations are anonymized in the results. The figures should therefore be treated as reported project savings—not verified realized savings or evidence of a typical result.[3][5][7]

For procurement, ask each vendor for a paid pilot on completed or active jobs and require a baseline plus measured results: estimator/precon hours, leveling discrepancies found, submittal/RFI turnaround, change-order exposure identified before award, and bid outcomes across a defined sample. Without that, “win-rate lift” and “rework avoided” claims are difficult to compare.
