## Response (verbatim)

````
Documented results exist, but the evidence is **uneven**: the available named-customer examples are mostly vendor-published case studies, and several concern large enterprises rather than mid-sized companies. The figures below should be treated as reported outcomes—not independently verified, comparable benchmarks.

| Provider / customer | Reported result | What it does—and does not—show |
|---|---|---|
| **HighRadius — Konica Minolta** | Reported a **20% improvement in forecast accuracy**, **15% lower cash-flow volatility**, **$400,000 in interest savings** on 90-day short-term loans, and **$30 million in working capital freed**. A separate report says Konica Minolta reduced DSO by **nine days**.[1][10] | Strongest example here spanning forecasting, financing and working capital; sources do not specify a forecast-accuracy baseline or establish that the company is mid-sized. |
| **HighRadius — Danone North America** | Reported **96% cash-forecast accuracy** and **$20 million annually recovered in invalid deductions**.[10] | Forecast accuracy is stated, but the source does not specify the forecast horizon or a before-and-after comparison. |
| **HighRadius — DXP** | Reported **$20 million added to cash flow**, a **20-day DSO reduction**, and **40% higher collector productivity**.[10] | Quantifies working-capital outcomes, but the cited report does not attribute all results specifically to ML forecasting. |
| **Kyriba — Cenveo** | Kyriba reports **93% cash-forecast accuracy** and **90% higher productivity**, with **$490,000 in value unlocked in less than three months**.[5] | A named company with quantified results; the source does not report idle cash reduced, interest savings, or DSO/DPO changes. |
| **Kyriba — Varsity Brands** | Reported **over 90% forecast accuracy** and **100% cash visibility**.[5] | Useful forecasting and visibility metrics; no quantified working-capital or financing outcome is given. |
| **Kyriba-enabled Amazon ML project** | Amazon reported reducing cash-positioning work from **up to eight hours to under 30 minutes** and developing a global 60-day forecast.[7] | Explicitly ML-based, but Amazon is not a mid-sized-company example; no forecast-accuracy percentage or cash savings is reported. |

**Bottom line:** Among the results available here, Konica Minolta offers the clearest quantified interest and working-capital claims; DXP offers a 20-day DSO reduction; and Cenveo and Varsity Brands provide forecast-accuracy figures. The cited material does **not** give a quantified reduction in idle cash or a DPO improvement for these examples. It also does not substantiate comparable customer outcomes for **Trovata, Cashforce, Agicap, or Nilus**, so no cross-vendor ranking or claim about typical mid-market impact is supportable from this evidence.
````
