## Response (verbatim)

````
Companies are using **auto-QA** to transcribe and score every call against configurable quality and compliance criteria, then turn flagged moments and recurring patterns into coaching, live agent prompts, and operational changes. Reported outcomes include full interaction coverage, fewer compliance incidents, shorter handle times, higher conversions, and less manual review—but the figures below are vendor- or case-study-reported, not independently comparable.

| Vendor | How it is used | Reported results |
|---|---|---|
| **Observe.AI** | Automatically evaluates interactions against QA criteria and flags potential compliance issues for review and coaching. | The vendor reports 100% interaction monitoring and, across its enterprise customer base, 23% lower average handle time (AHT), 10% higher conversions, 13% higher revenue, and a 97% improvement in compliance monitoring.[8] |
| **Level AI** | Applies contextual conversation analysis and automated scoring to interactions, helping teams identify coaching needs and compliance patterns. | The available result describes analysis of 100% of customer interactions, but does not provide a quantified customer outcome for the named measures.[9] |
| **CallMiner** | Analyzes voice and text conversations for QA, agent performance, and compliance, with real-time and post-call insights. | The available result describes its capabilities but does not provide a quantified customer outcome.[9] |
| **Cresta** | Scores conversations and offers real-time behavioral guidance, scorecards, and coaching recommendations. | The available result describes its capabilities but does not provide a quantified customer outcome.[9] |
| **Balto** | Combines live agent guidance—such as reminders for required disclosures—with post-call automated QA and coaching. | Balto says its automated QA can score 100% of interactions against compliance scorecards; the available result does not provide a quantified customer outcome.[4] |
| **MaestroQA** | Uses configurable QA scorecards and coaching workflows to help teams assess and improve agent performance. | The available result describes its capabilities but does not provide a quantified customer outcome.[4][9] |

A separate 2024 insurance case study reports **100% automated coverage** for compliance and sentiment, **67% lower AHT for new agents**, and a **38% improvement across interactions** after AI-powered real-time assistance; it also says automated assessment freed QA staff to focus more on improvement work, but gives no dollar or staffing-savings figure.[2] Another case study reports **100% call coverage, 30% fewer compliance incidents, and 80% less time spent on manual reviews**; the result excerpt does not identify the vendor, so it should not be attributed to one of the named platforms.[6]

In practice, these systems expand visibility beyond small manual samples: they score calls, surface evidence behind low or risky scores, and help supervisors prioritize coaching or investigate exceptions. Reported “100% coverage” means automated analysis of interactions—not necessarily that humans review every call or that every automated score has been validated.[2][4][8]
````
