# Script 01 — "What is Orama Zetta?"

| | |
|---|---|
| **Runtime** | ~80 seconds |
| **Audience** | Everyone (the anchor explainer) |
| **Purpose** | In 80 seconds: what it is, who it's for, why it matters |
| **Tone** | Calm, confident, plain-spoken. No hype. |
| **Music** | Sparse, building; resolves on the tagline |
| **Distribution** | Public-safe |
| **Source brief** | [`../briefs/01-what-is-orama-zetta.md`](../briefs/01-what-is-orama-zetta.md) |

---

### SCENE 1 — COLD OPEN  [0:00–0:10]
**VISUAL:** A single laptop on a desk, late. A cursor blinks in an empty query box. Pull back slowly — around it, dim, a wall of dashboards and database logos (Snowflake, Postgres, a CRM).
**VO:** "Your company has the data. And the questions. What it doesn't have is enough people who can turn one into the other."
**SUPER:** *Orama Zetta*

### SCENE 2 — THE WAIT  [0:10–0:20]
**VISUAL:** A queue of chat requests / tickets stacking up behind one tired engineer. Time-lapse: the stack grows; the engineer doesn't move.
**VO:** "So everyone waits — in line, behind the few people who can wire the data, build the model, and deploy it."
**SFX:** A soft, accumulating tick per ticket.

### SCENE 3 — THE TURN  [0:20–0:34]
**VISUAL:** The dim wall snaps into focus as the Orama Zetta desktop app. A hand types one sentence: *"Score every active account for churn risk — and tell me why."* Beat. Then: a file — `churn-workflow.json` — dragged into the same window.
**VO:** "Orama Zetta changes who can do that. Describe what you're after, in plain language — or hand over a workflow you already built."
**SUPER:** *Plain language → or your existing artifacts*

### SCENE 4 — IT ARCHITECTS  [0:34–0:48]
**VISUAL:** From the sentence, a clean execution graph assembles node by node — *connect → clean → model → index → serve*. Each node ticks green as a validation pass runs across it.
**VO:** "It designs the whole thing — correctly. Connect the data, clean it, train real models and pipelines on your data, serve the answer — and it shows you every step it took. If you're not training on your data, you're not fully using AI."
**SUPER:** *Correct by construction*

### SCENE 5 — THREE DISCIPLINES, ONE WORKFLOW  [0:48–0:58]
**VISUAL:** Three labeled silhouettes — PLATFORM ENGINEER · DATA ENGINEER · DATA SCIENTIST — slide together and merge into the single app icon.
**VO:** "The platform engineer, the data engineer, the data scientist — three disciplines, one app."

### SCENE 6 — DEPLOY ON YOUR TURF  [0:58–1:08]
**VISUAL:** One "Deploy" click. A topology map: nodes light up inside a labeled boundary — *Your cloud · Your cluster · Your firewall*. A small check appears: *0 bytes left your environment.*
**VO:** "Then it deploys — on infrastructure you control. Your data never leaves your walls. The work comes to the data."

### SCENE 7 — THE PAYOFF  [1:08–1:18]
**VISUAL:** Answers stream back with a lineage trail (sources → steps → freshness). A calendar in the corner flips from "quarter" to "minutes."
**VO:** "What used to take a quarter takes minutes — production-grade AI, deployed on your turf."

### SCENE 8 — TAGLINE  [1:18–1:22]
**VISUAL:** Logo. Tagline types on.
**VO:** "Orama Zetta. Intelligence velocity — from question to answer, in minutes. For big thinkers."
**SUPER:** *Orama Zetta — Intelligence Velocity for Big Thinkers*

---

**VO word count:** ~170 words (~85s with pauses). **Cut-down (30s):** Scenes 1, 3, 6, 8.
