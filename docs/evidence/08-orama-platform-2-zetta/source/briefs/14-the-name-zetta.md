# 14 — The name: Zetta

**Logline:** A zettabyte is one billion terabytes. The name is a promise about the ceiling: build for the largest data problems on Earth, and yours are easy.

**For:** everyone — but it lands hardest with technical buyers and investors (a credibility + ambition beat).

---

**Zetta** comes from **zettabyte** — 10²¹ bytes, one *billion* terabytes, the scale of all the data humanity is producing. It's the unit past exa, near the top of the ladder. We didn't pick it because your data is that big. We picked it because of a one-way truth about scale: **going bigger is the hard direction; going smaller is free.**

An engine designed for the extreme — the world's first *adaptive* database — has nothing but headroom at normal scale. The architecture that survives a billion terabytes — data that lives where it sits, work that runs next to it, storage that tiers from memory to cold and back — is exactly the architecture that's *fast and cheap* at the terabytes or gigabytes you actually run, because at your scale it's barely trying. The hard problems force the right design; the easy ones inherit it for free.

So the name is two things at once. A **ceiling you'll never hit** — you don't outgrow Zetta; it was overbuilt for your scale on purpose. And a **principle**: *if you can solve very large data problems, you can solve small ones too.* That's the order we build in — top down — which is why the small ones feel effortless.

> Honest framing (for script-writers): this is the design *ambition* and *principle*, not a claim that the engine moves a zettabyte today. Don't quote throughput numbers as shipped — see [`../roadmap.md`](../roadmap.md).

**Talking points**
- Zetta = zettabyte = one billion terabytes (10²¹) — the top of the scale ladder.
- Scale is one-way: bigger is hard, smaller is free. Design for the ceiling, inherit the floor.
- The architecture that survives the extreme (data locality, compute-near-data, tiered storage) is *why* it's fast + cheap at normal scale.
- You never outgrow it — overbuilt for your scale by design.
- It's the build order: top down. Solve the hardest, the rest comes free.
- The engine that earns the name is the world's first *adaptive* database — built for the ceiling, fast and cheap at the floor.

**Lines worth saying**
- "A zettabyte is one billion terabytes. We didn't name it for your data. We named it for the ceiling."
- "If you can solve very large data problems, you can solve small ones too."
- "You don't outgrow Zetta. It was overbuilt for your scale on purpose."
