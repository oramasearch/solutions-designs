# 16 — Auto-ANI, not AGI

**Logline:** Everyone's chasing AGI. What a company actually needs is **auto-ANI** — automated, artificial *narrow* intelligence: production-grade, specialized to one job and your data, optimized for cost, governance, and personalized accuracy.

**For:** technical buyers, platform/AI leads, and investors — the people who have to ship the thing, not just admire the frontier.

---

AGI is the broad, general-purpose chase — one model that can do anything. An enterprise problem is the opposite of general. Churn, fraud, demand, lead scoring, predictive maintenance — each is a single, narrow, well-defined task. That's **ANI** — artificial *narrow* intelligence, specialized to one job — and it's exactly what the business needs. You don't need a system that can do everything; you need one that does *your* thing, correctly, in production.

**Auto-ANI** is the platform automating the build of that narrow intelligence end to end — featurize, train, explain, deploy, govern, run — without staffing a team for each one. The "auto" is the velocity; the "ANI" is the scope. Together they're the honest version of "enterprise AI": not a general brain you rent and prompt, but a specialized, production-grade system Orama Zetta builds for one job on your data.

What decides whether narrow intelligence actually *ships* is three axes, and auto-ANI is optimized for all three. **Cost:** heterogeneous compute, tiered storage, and burst only when needed — plus use-case-specific, fused GPU kernels in C++ for transformational cost profiles, so the unit economics work at production scale instead of a demo. **Governance:** it runs inside your walls, identity-scoped, and audited — narrow intelligence you can actually let loose because you can see and control what it touches. **Personalized accuracy:** it's *trained on your data*, so it has real predictive power for your business, not transfer-learned guesses from the open web. If you're not training on your data, you're not fully using AI (see [`15-learning-from-your-data.md`](15-learning-from-your-data.md)).

That's the whole pitch in one line: the industry is selling the dream of general intelligence; the company that wins ships *narrow* intelligence — automated, cheap, governed, and accurate on its own data. **Intelligence Velocity for big thinkers** (brief [`04`](04-intelligence-velocity.md)) is how fast auto-ANI goes from intent to running.

> Honest framing (for script-writers): the cost axis leans on the fused-kernel / tiered-storage substrate, much of which is **in flight** — see [`../roadmap.md`](../roadmap.md) and [`06-orama-zetta-spine-and-substrate.md`](06-orama-zetta-spine-and-substrate.md) before claiming the cost numbers as shipped.

**Talking points**
- AGI is general; an enterprise problem is *narrow* — one task, one definition, one outcome. ANI fits it.
- **Auto-ANI** = the platform automates building that narrow, production-grade intelligence end to end.
- Three axes decide whether it ships: **cost**, **governance**, **personalized accuracy** — auto-ANI optimizes all three.
- Cost: heterogeneous compute + tiered storage + burst-on-demand + fused GPU kernels → transformational cost profiles.
- Governance: in your walls, identity-scoped, audited.
- Accuracy: trained on *your* data — real predictive power, not a rented-model guess.

**Lines worth saying**
- "Everyone's chasing AGI. You need auto-ANI."
- "Artificial narrow intelligence — automated, specialized to your job, trained on your data."
- "Narrow is the point. Your business isn't a general problem."
- "Cost, governance, accuracy — that's what decides whether intelligence ships. We optimize all three."
- "If you're not training on your data, you're not fully using AI."
