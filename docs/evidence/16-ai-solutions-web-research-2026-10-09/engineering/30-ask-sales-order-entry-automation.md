# 30-ask-sales-order-entry-automation

- **Tool:** `mcp__perplexity__perplexity_ask` (search_context_size: high)
- **Query:** What documented results exist for AI that automates sales order entry from customer emails, PDFs and faxed purchase orders into ERP at distributors and manufacturers (vendors such as Conexiom, Rossum, Hypatos, Esker, Lido, Tray, Endeavor, Turian, Pallet)? Name customers and quantify orders processed, touchless rate, order entry time, error rate, CSR headcount redeployed.
- **Timestamp (UTC):** 2026-10-09T20:42:34.048Z

## Response (verbatim)

````
Documented public results are strongest for Esker, Rossum and Conexiom. The figures below come from vendor or case-study materials, so treat them as reported outcomes—not independently audited comparisons. “Touchless” also varies by source: it may mean no CSR intervention, no data changes, or an automated step in the workflow.

| Vendor / customer | Documented results |
|---|---|
| **Conexiom — Standard Electric** | Up to **80%** of orders processed without CSR intervention; order processing was **88% faster** (quotes reportedly fell from as much as 40 minutes to under 5); **100% data accuracy** was reported. Staff were freed for higher-value customer service, but no redeployed headcount or order volume was given.[1] |
| **Esker — MCR Safety** | Touchless automation reached **91%**; average processing time fell from **12–24 hours to under one hour**; average monthly order-entry errors fell from **75 to 28**. Before automation, 14 employees processed about 22 orders per day; afterward, 10 processed about 27 per day—a **four-person staffing reduction** alongside higher throughput. The case study does not say whether those employees were redeployed or eliminated.[2] |
| **Esker — MEMC** | The case study reports **at least 35 hours per week** of manual entry eliminated—over **1,800 hours annually**, roughly one full-time equivalent. MEMC received hundreds of orders and changes daily, but the published material describes expected benefits and targets rather than measured touchless, cycle-time or error-rate results.[3] |
| **Esker — HealthCo** | Reported a **50% higher and rising touchless rate**, **40% increase to 100% electronic order throughput**, nearly **60% lower order-entry labor overhead**, and over **40% lower monthly processing cost**. The case study does not provide an absolute final touchless percentage or headcount figure.[5] |
| **Esker — Terumo** | Order processing time decreased **60%** versus manual ERP processing. The company maintained its staffing level despite rising order volume, freeing customer-care time for other work; it had taught touchless rules for **575 customer orders**. No absolute touchless rate or error reduction was reported.[7] |
| **Rossum — allnex** | By the end of 2025, the global rollout reportedly reached **70% automation** and **98% data-extraction accuracy**. At earlier stages, automation reached 77% in the Americas in December 2023 and 80% across the Americas and EMEA by December 2024. Order volumes and staffing outcomes were not stated.[9] |
| **Rossum — LAPP** | AI correctly captured **94.9% of order data fields**. The case study says exceptions were intentionally left for manual handling; it does not report a touchless rate, processing-time reduction, order volume or headcount impact.[10] |

Esker also publishes platform-wide figures—**2.59 million orders automated in a month**, **400+ customer organizations**, **74% of orders fully automated with no changes required**, and **72% fewer manual order-processing errors**—but these are aggregate vendor metrics, not results for a named distributor or manufacturer.[8]

For the other vendors named—Hypatos, Lido, Tray, Endeavor and Turian—the available results here do not substantiate comparable named-customer sales-order outcomes. In particular, there is not enough published evidence in these materials to quantify their orders processed, touchless rates, entry times, error rates or CSR redeployment.
````
