### What I could substantiate—and what I could not

The available sources do **not** establish well-supported, named-and-quantified production results for markdown optimization, planogram optimization, store labor scheduling, shrink/loss prediction, fresh-food waste reduction, returns prediction, or new-store site selection. For those categories, a useful evidence standard is to require a named retailer or CPG company, a defined deployment scope and period, a baseline/control, and a reported financial or operational outcome—not just model accuracy or claimed savings.
