# 01 — perplexity_research — sales operations and pricing

- **Tool:** `mcp__perplexity__perplexity_research`
- **Query:** What machine learning solutions are deployed in B2B sales operations and pricing beyond lead scoring and churn: CPQ price optimization and deal guidance, win/loss prediction, deal desk approval automation, quota and territory planning, sales forecasting (pipeline-based revenue forecast)? For each, give named vendor or company case studies with measured ROI (margin points, win-rate lift, forecast accuracy) and how mid-market companies buy them.
- **Timestamp (UTC):** 2026-10-09T20:16:26Z

```
| Solution | Named vendor or company case studies with measured ROI | How mid-market companies buy them |
|---|---|---|
| **CPQ price optimization and deal guidance** | Models recommend deal-specific target and floor prices; the quoting system applies commercial guardrails. **Dell Technologies + Vendavo** reports **100+ basis points of margin improvement**; 90% of quotes go out within four hours. In a separate **Pricefx** deployment at an unnamed packaging company, AI-optimized guidance was associated with a **238-basis-point gross-margin lift** in year one. These are margin outcomes, not measured win-rate lifts.[web:76][web:168] | Buy a pricing module alongside—or connected to—the existing CPQ rather than assuming CPQ itself optimizes prices. **Pricefx** offers a modular starting point and sends guidance into existing CRM, ERP and CPQ systems; **Zilliant Price IQ** can publish guidance into those systems, including through a Salesforce CPQ connector. Both are integration-led purchases rather than a published, self-service mid-market price.[web:226][web:92][web:95] |
| **Win/loss prediction for open opportunities** | Deal models score the likelihood that an *opportunity* will close and flag risk; this is distinct from post-deal win/loss interviews. **Experian + Gong AI Deal Predictor** reports a **25% increase in win rates** and **10% growth in sales volume** after using deal signals to prioritize and intervene. The case does not isolate the predictor’s effect from the broader Gong rollout.[web:1] | A mid-market team can add predictive deal intelligence to its CRM workflow rather than build a model. **Gong** sells customized proposals with **per-user licenses plus a platform fee**, based on selected capabilities and team size; it supports CRM-connected deal analysis.[web:213][web:129] |
| **Deal-desk approval automation** | The deployed pattern combines pricing intelligence with **policy-based** discount limits, exception routing and audit trails—not an ML model independently authorizing discounts. **Zapier + DealHub AI** reduced approval turnaround from **multiple days to within eight hours** by replacing sequential handoffs with configured, parallel approvals. **Dell + Vendavo** reports that **75% of quotes no longer require pricing approval**, alongside its margin gain above.[web:3][web:76] | Buy this as a **CPQ/deal-desk workflow** connected to the CRM. DealHub’s quote-based **Growth** package includes CPQ, parallel approvals and a deal-desk dashboard, with native Salesforce, HubSpot and Dynamics integrations. The buyer still defines which exceptions require human approval.[web:211][web:3] |
| **Quota and territory planning** | This category mixes propensity signals and AI-assisted scenario planning with **constraint optimization**; a faster territory plan is not, by itself, proof of an ML-driven quota lift. **Pure Storage + Anaplan Optimizer** reports that finding options for **500+ territories and account assignments fell from days to minutes**, using account data and planning constraints. **Udemy + Fullcast** reports an **80% reduction in annual planning time**, from months to weeks; its case does not establish that AI specifically caused that saving.[web:52][web:63] | Start with a planning module that connects account, attainment and CRM data. **Fullcast Standard** includes AI-powered territory balancing and bidirectional Salesforce sync. For a published-price alternative, **Salesforce Sales Planning** lists territory and quota planning at **$75 per user per month, billed annually**, available as an add-on or within specified suites.[web:121][web:196] |
| **Pipeline-based revenue forecasting** | Forecasting tools combine opportunity history, pipeline changes and deal signals to project revenue, rather than relying solely on reps’ stage-weighted estimates. **LaunchDarkly + Aviso** reports **98%+ forecast accuracy** with predictive forecasting and pipeline analytics. **Fortinet + Clari** reports **97% forecast accuracy** in its customer case study, which also describes use of historical trends and predictive insights. Neither figure is a guaranteed accuracy level for another company.[web:33][web:43] | Buy a forecast/revenue-intelligence subscription integrated with the existing CRM, then configure forecast categories and historical pipeline data. **Aviso** offers predictive forecasting and deal insights as a vendor-led purchase; **Clari** is custom-quoted, with module and user count affecting the subscription. A team needing basic CRM-native forecasting can instead assess **HubSpot Sales Hub Professional**, which publishes a **$90-per-seat monthly price billed annually**.[web:33][web:123][web:204] |

**Evidence caveat:** These are vendor-published customer outcomes from different deployments—not controlled, comparable ROI estimates. In particular, approval routing and territory optimization often use explicit rules or mathematical optimization even when sold within an “AI” platform.[web:3][web:52]
```
