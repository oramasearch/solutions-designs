### What to expect the AI to decide

- **Urgency:** Usually scored against emergency definitions, property policies, and escalation rules; human escalation remains available for exceptions.[3][8]
- **Troubleshooting:** Guided steps may resolve simple issues or gather details before dispatch.[1][3]
- **Trade/vendor:** Systems can recommend or select a trade/vendor based on the issue and preferred-vendor list; actual auto-dispatch depends on configuration and product tier.[1][2]
- **Cost approval:** Vendoroo advertises customizable workflows and approvals, but public materials do not establish a universal automatic dollar limit. Ask vendors to demonstrate how estimates above your threshold are held for approval.[1]
- **Results and pricing:** Compare the same metrics across vendors—cost per completed work order, median time to resolution, dispatches avoided per 100 requests, and after-hours response—using your own portfolio baseline. Public figures often reflect vendor-reported outcomes rather than independently audited head-to-head studies.
