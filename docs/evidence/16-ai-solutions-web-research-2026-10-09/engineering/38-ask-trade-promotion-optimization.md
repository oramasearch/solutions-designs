# 38-ask-trade-promotion-optimization

- **Tool:** `mcp__perplexity__perplexity_ask` (search_context_size: high)
- **Query:** What documented results exist for ML-based trade promotion optimization and promotion post-event analysis for small and mid-sized CPG brands (e.g. vendors Vividly, Cresicor, Kantar/ Periscope, Wiser, Antuit, BluePlanner, Exceedra, Tiger Analytics, Mu Sigma)? Quantify trade spend ROI, incremental lift, deduction recovery, with named brands.
- **Timestamp (UTC):** 2026-10-09T20:46:31.840Z

## Response (verbatim)

````
The strongest **named, quantified public results in the available evidence are from Vividly**. The evidence is much thinner for incremental promotion lift than for deduction recovery, forecasting, or workflow efficiency—and it does **not** support a reliable vendor-by-vendor comparison across all the companies listed.

| Brand / result | What is documented | Qualification |
|---|---|---|
| **Health-Ade — Vividly** | Health-Ade says it disputed **$1.1 million** in deductions in 2024 and recovered **$700,000**; Vividly also reports that deduction-processing time was cut in half.[6][4] | Customer-reported recovery and time result; not a measured promotion-lift or trade-spend ROI figure. |
| **Jovial Foods — Vividly** | Vividly reports **more than $27,000** recovered in invalid chargebacks/deductions.[2][6] | Vendor-published case result; no incremental sales-lift figure shown. |
| **Perfect Snacks — Vividly** | Vividly describes improved forecasting accuracy, more granular promotion reporting, and improved trade-promotion ROI, but the available result does **not** quantify the ROI improvement.[2][4] | Qualitative case result. |
| **Perfect Snacks — Vividly** | A separate Vividly page reports a **23% improvement in forecasting accuracy**.[2] | Forecast accuracy is not the same as promotional lift or ROI. |
| **Quinn Snacks — Vividly** | Vividly reports hours of manual work saved and more focused trade spending, without a numerical sales-lift or ROI result.[2][4] | Qualitative case result. |
| **Vividly users — aggregate analysis** | Vividly says its customers were **15.8% more likely** than non-users to improve incremental ROI from 2024 to 2025, and had **8% higher overall sales growth**.[1] | Vendor analysis across nearly 9,000 CPG brands; the cited summary does not provide enough detail to establish a causal effect or isolate small/mid-sized brands. |

**What can—and cannot—be concluded:** The clearest named-brand financial outcome is Health-Ade’s reported $700,000 recovery. The results found do not provide a named SMB brand’s measured incremental promotion lift or a directly comparable trade-spend ROI percentage. Forecast accuracy, recovered deductions, labor savings, and incremental promotional lift are different measures and should not be treated as interchangeable.

For **Cresicor, Kantar/Periscope, Wiser, Antuit, BluePlanner, Exceedra, Tiger Analytics, and Mu Sigma**, the available search evidence here does not establish comparable named-brand, quantified outcomes. That means **no substantiated figure can be offered from these results**, not that those vendors have no case studies. Vividly’s $700,000 recovery and other figures are vendor-published/customer-attributed claims, rather than independently verified head-to-head results.[2][4][6]
````
