# 20-ask-delivery-date-promise

- **Tool:** `mcp__perplexity__perplexity_ask` (search_context_size: high)
- **Query:** What results have retailers, e-commerce brands and distributors reported from ML-based delivery date promise / estimated delivery date prediction at checkout (e.g. Wayfair, Zalando, Shopify Promise, Narvar, ParcelPerform, project44)? Quantify the effect on conversion rate, WISMO (where is my order) support contacts, on-time rate, and cancellations. Is this used by mid-market e-commerce and B2B distributors?
- **Timestamp (UTC):** 2026-10-09T20:41:15.977Z

## Response (verbatim)

````
**The best-supported reported results are positive, but the numbers are not directly comparable.** Public case studies show conversion gains from presenting more specific, personalized delivery dates; the clearest named-company examples are Wayfair and Narvar customers. Most vendors report WISMO reductions from a combined package of accurate dates, tracking, and proactive notifications—not from checkout prediction alone. Public figures for on-time delivery and cancellations are sparse.

| Company or product | Reported result | What it does—and doesn’t—show |
|---|---|---|
| **Wayfair** | Its engineering team said a new prediction model cut the gap between promised and actual delivery time roughly in half while keeping its on-time delivery rate comparable to the previous model; the company reported higher conversion and revenue but did not publish a conversion percentage.[1] | A direct model comparison, but no disclosed conversion lift or cancellation/WISMO results. |
| **Narvar Promise: Harry Rosen** | Narvar reports over 90% delivery-date accuracy, a 13% conversion lift, and 16% fewer WISMO calls after showing predicted dates on product pages and at checkout.[8] | A retailer-specific case study. The source does not quantify on-time rate or cancellations. |
| **Narvar Promise: IWA Wine Accessories** | Narvar reports 19% higher product-page conversion, 10% higher checkout conversion, over 95% date accuracy, and 47% fewer WISMO inquiries.[6] | The WISMO improvement followed accurate dates **and proactive post-purchase communication**, so it cannot be attributed to prediction alone. |
| **Shopify Shop Promise** | Shopify says eligible merchants have seen conversion increases of up to 25% when a Shop Promise delivery date is displayed.[4] | “Up to” is a ceiling, not a typical or independently reported average; eligibility requires strong shipping performance. |
| **Narvar platform claims** | Narvar advertises up to 5% higher conversion from AI-powered delivery estimates, 95%+ EDD accuracy, and up to 50–60% fewer WISMO inquiries from proactive updates.[5] | Vendor-wide claims, not a single controlled case study; WISMO reduction is tied to notifications/tracking as well as date accuracy. |
| **Zalando, ParcelPerform, project44** | The available results did not provide a quantified, attributable checkout-prediction outcome for Zalando or ParcelPerform. A project44 case cited by a secondary source reports UrbanStems reduced WISMO calls by 63%, but that involved shipment data and proactive tracking updates, not just the checkout promise.[4][7] | Treat the UrbanStems figure as a post-purchase visibility result, not an EDD-model-only effect. |

### What can be said about each metric

- **Conversion:** Public examples range from Narvar’s 10% checkout lift at IWA to 13% at Harry Rosen; Shopify reports “up to 25%” for eligible Shop Promise merchants.[6][8][4] These are company-reported results with differing designs and baselines, so they should not be treated as a single expected uplift.
- **WISMO:** Reported reductions include 16% at Harry Rosen and 47% at IWA Wine Accessories; Narvar advertises up to 50–60% for proactive updates.[8][6][5] These results commonly combine better estimates with tracking and customer notifications.
- **On-time delivery:** Wayfair’s published result is that it made delivery promises about twice as precise—halving the gap between promised and actual timing—while maintaining a comparable delivery rate.[1] The sources provide few comparable on-time-rate percentages.
- **Cancellations:** The cited case studies do not disclose a reliable cancellation reduction attributable to ML delivery-date prediction.

### Is this used by mid-market e-commerce and B2B distributors?

**Yes, the practice is not limited to the largest retailers.** Narvar’s IWA Wine Accessories and Harry Rosen examples show adoption beyond mega-marketplaces, and Shopify offers delivery-date promises to eligible merchants on its platform.[6][8][4] But the public evidence is much thinner for **B2B distributors**: the results here do not establish how widely they use checkout-level ML promises or provide a quantified distributor case.

For a buyer evaluating this, the useful distinction is between (1) **checkout EDD prediction**, which may influence conversion and promise accuracy, and (2) **post-purchase tracking and proactive exception updates**, which are more directly tied to WISMO reduction. Ask vendors for controlled results on conversion, promise accuracy/on-time performance, WISMO per order, and cancellation rate, with the baseline and test period disclosed.
````
