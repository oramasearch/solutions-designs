# 33-ask-returns-reduction

- **Tool:** `mcp__perplexity__perplexity_ask` (search_context_size: high)
- **Query:** What documented results exist for ML that predicts and reduces e-commerce product returns (return-risk prediction at checkout, size and fit recommendation, return fraud and wardrobing detection, returns routing/disposition) at retailers like Zalando, ASOS, Stitch Fix, Happy Returns, Narvar, Loop Returns, True Fit, Fit Analytics, Appriss Retail? Quantify return-rate reduction and cost savings, with named mid-market brands where possible.

## Response (verbatim)

````
Documented results are strongest for **size/fit recommendations**. Evidence is much thinner for checkout return-risk scoring, fraud/wardrobing detection, and automated disposition—especially for named mid-market brands and independently verified cost savings.

### Quantified examples

| Company or brand | Reported result | What it does—and evidence caveat |
|---|---:|---|
| **M&Co / True Fit** | 9.8% lower return rate over six months among customers who bought the recommended size[3] | Fit recommendation; the figure is for users who followed the recommendation, not necessarily a sitewide reduction. The available source describes it as a vendor-adjacent case-study claim.[3] |
| **Moosejaw / True Fit** | 24% fewer size-bracketing behaviors; True Fit also reports 24% fewer fit-related returns[4][7][9] | Fit guidance aimed at shoppers adding multiple sizes. These are vendor-reported results; sources vary in whether they describe bracketing or returns.[7][9] |
| **APL / True Fit** | 15% fewer fit-related returns[7] | Vendor-reported fit recommendation result; no cost savings are given.[7] |
| **ASICS / True Fit** | 50% fewer size-bracketing behaviors[3] | A specific behavior metric, not a 50% drop in overall returns.[3] |
| **Silver Jeans Co / True Fit** | A True Fit customer-reference page lists a case study, but the available result does not state a return-reduction figure.[5] | Useful named-brand reference, but no quantified returns outcome in the available material.[5] |
| **Zalando, ASOS, Stitch Fix** | No verifiable, quantified return-reduction result for the requested ML use cases appeared in the available results. | Avoid treating general AI, personalization, or styling claims as evidence of lower returns. |

True Fit markets up to **40% fewer fit-related returns** when shoppers use its guidance, and reports results from A/B tests, but that is a vendor-wide “up to” claim rather than a named-brand, independently audited outcome.[9] Another True Fit customer-reference page reports an 18% reduction in returns, but the snippet does not identify the retailer or provide enough context to compare it with the named cases.[5]

### Other use cases and vendors

- **Checkout return-risk prediction:** The available sources did not establish quantified deployments or named mid-market case studies for Zalando, ASOS, Narvar, Loop Returns, or the other named companies.
- **Fraud and wardrobing detection:** No quantified case-study evidence was present for Appriss Retail or the other named vendors. Fraud prevention may reduce abuse losses, but that should not be presented as a lower customer return rate without a measured result.
- **Returns routing and disposition:** Happy Returns’ source discusses convenience and reverse-logistics efficiency, but provides no quantified savings or brand-level outcome in the available excerpt.[1] Narvar’s return services are described as improving the returns experience; no measured cost or return-rate reduction is reported there.[1]
- **Loop Returns, Fit Analytics, and Appriss Retail:** No substantiated quantified outcome for the requested categories appeared in the available results.

### Bottom line

The clearest quantified evidence here is **fit guidance reducing fit-related returns or multi-size bracketing**, with named examples including M&Co, Moosejaw, APL, and ASICS. The numbers are mostly vendor-reported and often apply to shoppers who use the recommendation—not the entire retailer. The available results do **not** support a defensible dollar-savings estimate or a broad claim that return-prediction, fraud-detection, or disposition ML has reduced total returns at the named retailers.
````
