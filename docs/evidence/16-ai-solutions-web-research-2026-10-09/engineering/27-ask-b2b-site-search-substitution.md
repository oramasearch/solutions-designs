# 27-ask-b2b-site-search-substitution

- **Tool:** `mcp__perplexity__perplexity_ask` (search_context_size: high)
- **Query:** What results do industrial distributors and B2B e-commerce companies report from ML/AI-powered site search relevance, product substitution recommendations for out-of-stock items, and cross-sell recommendations (e.g. Grainger, Fastenal, MSC, Ferguson, Würth, Zoro, using Algolia, Coveo, Bloomreach, Constructor, Lucidworks, Unbxd)? Quantify conversion lift, revenue per search, zero-result rate, average order value.
- **Timestamp (UTC):** 2026-10-09T20:42:00.955Z

## Response (verbatim)

````
Public evidence is **thin for named industrial distributors**: few disclose audited, vendor-attributed results specifically for substitution or cross-sell. The clearest quantified industrial example found is RS Group—not Grainger, Fastenal, MSC, Ferguson, Würth, or Zoro—and it reports search conversion and null-search improvements, but not revenue per search or AOV.

| Publicly reported example | Search conversion | Zero/null results | Revenue per search | AOV |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| **RS Group** using Rezolve AI/GroupBy | Up **more than 3.2%** | Down **47%** | Not reported | Not reported |
| **Hercules Sealing Products** using Coveo | Web revenue grew, but no numerical lift disclosed | Not quantified | Not reported | Not reported |
| **HumCommerce’s generalized industrial-distributor example** | **12% boost** in search-traffic conversion | Down **35%** | Not reported | Up **18%** from smart add-ons |
| **B2B industrial search example** in an ecommerce best-practices article | Search-driven add-to-cart rate doubled | Not reported | Not reported | Not reported |

RS Group also said its search-rule count fell from over 30,000 to zero, while null searches declined 47% and search conversion rose more than 3.2%[4]. The Coveo case for Hercules Sealing Products describes revenue growth but gives no baseline or percentage, so it cannot support a precise lift estimate[1]. HumCommerce reports a 12% conversion boost, 35% fewer zero-result searches, and 18% higher AOV for an industrial-distributor example, but does not identify the company or provide methodology; treat these as vendor/industry claims rather than independently verified benchmarks[3]. Another industry example reports doubled search-driven add-to-cart rate, without naming the distributor or giving revenue/AOV metrics[8].

**For the specific tactics asked about:**

- **Search relevance:** The strongest named industrial figure in these results is RS Group’s **>3.2% increase in search conversion** and **47% reduction in null searches**[4]. This is a reported increase, not clearly specified as relative versus percentage-point lift.
- **Out-of-stock substitutions:** No quantified public result for an industrial distributor’s substitution recommendations appeared in the available evidence.
- **Cross-sell:** The only industrial-specific numeric result located is the generalized claim of **18% higher AOV** from smart add-ons; it is not a named-company case study[3].
- **Revenue per search:** No credible, named industrial-distributor result with a numeric revenue-per-search lift appeared in the results.

**Bottom line:** A defensible public benchmark from this evidence is a modest, explicitly reported search-conversion gain (**>3.2%**) alongside a substantial reduction in null searches (**47%**) at RS Group. Larger figures—roughly **12% conversion** and **18% AOV**—are available only as less transparent generalized claims, not as attributable results from the named distributors or platforms in the question[3][4].
````
