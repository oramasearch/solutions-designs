# Script 09 — "You're not slow. You're the bottleneck." (for the platform / AI lead)

| | |
|---|---|
| **Runtime** | ~70 seconds |
| **Audience** | Platform / AI leads at mid-to-large companies (the Charles persona) |
| **Purpose** | Name their exact pain, then show the way out without losing control |
| **Tone** | Peer-to-peer, knowing, a little wry. Engineer talking to engineer. |
| **Music** | Low, tense in the setup; lifts at "hand your devs the thing" |
| **Distribution** | Public-safe (no customer named) |
| **Source brief** | [`../briefs/09-for-the-platform-lead.md`](../briefs/09-for-the-platform-lead.md) |

---

### SCENE 1 — THE MIRROR  [0:00–0:12]
**VISUAL:** Over-the-shoulder on a lead at their desk; Slack is a wall of "quick question — can you pull…", "any update on the churn thing?", "blocked on you." They haven't typed a reply in minutes.
**VO:** "Every team wants intelligence from the company's data. And the only way they get it… is by waiting for you."
**SUPER:** *For the platform lead*

### SCENE 2 — NOT YOUR FAULT  [0:12–0:24]
**VISUAL:** Their hand-built setup on screen — an automation flow wired to a sidecar and a database, sticky notes, a "DO NOT TOUCH" label. It works. It's fragile. It's *theirs*.
**VO:** "You built the thing. It works. But only you can really run it — so it doesn't scale past your own hours. The business says you're too slow. You're not slow. You're one person standing in a queue."

### SCENE 3 — HAND THEM THE THING  [0:24–0:40]
**VISUAL:** Drag the automation flow into Orama Zetta → it re-architects into a clean graph, swapping the "LLM guess" node for *trained models and pipelines*. Then: a build artifact a developer downloads and runs themselves; the lead is no longer in the loop on the diagram.
**VO:** "Import what you already built. Orama Zetta re-architects it — correctly, with models and pipelines trained on your data where you had an LLM guessing — and hands your developers a build they run themselves. You're out of the middle."

### SCENE 4 — KEEP THE CONTROL  [0:40–0:54]
**VISUAL:** The lead's view: an architecture + governance panel — your SSO wired, access scoped, an audit log, an approval gate. Calm, in command.
**VO:** "You don't give up control. You own the architecture and the governance. You just stop being the manual step in front of every request."

### SCENE 5 — IN YOUR FIREWALL  [0:54–1:04]
**VISUAL:** Deploy → nodes light up inside the company boundary; *0 bytes left your environment.*
**VO:** "And it deploys inside your firewall, behind your SSO — one cluster, any cloud or on-prem. Your data never leaves."

### SCENE 6 — CTA  [1:04–1:12]
**VISUAL:** Logo + tagline. A single line of CTA text.
**VO:** "The thing you'd have spent a quarter productionizing — your team's using it this sprint. Orama Zetta. Intelligence velocity for big thinkers."
**SUPER:** *Get out of the bottleneck → [CTA]*

---

**VO word count:** ~170 words (~70s). **Note:** keep the automation+sidecar visual generic — do not show a real customer's workflow in a public cut (see [`13-the-motive-story.md`](13-the-motive-story.md) confidentiality note).
