**For the other requested applications:** the search results available for this answer did not substantiate named farms, cooperatives, agribusinesses, or vendors with independently reported, quantified ROI for grain-merchandising yield prediction, variable-rate seeding or fertilizer, irrigation scheduling, feed formulation, specialty-crop harvest and labor planning, or computer-vision grain grading. That is not evidence those systems do not exist; it means a defensible ROI claim needs a specific deployment source and a clear metric.

When evaluating claims in those areas, distinguish **model accuracy or estimated potential** from **measured operational impact**. Strong ROI evidence would report a baseline and post-deployment result—such as procurement forecast error and margin, yield or input cost per acre, water or energy saved, disease-treatment cost, feed cost per unit of milk or gain, harvest loss or labor hours, or grading throughput and rejection rate.
