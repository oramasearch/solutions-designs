# 01 — perplexity_research — construction and engineering

- **Tool:** `mcp__perplexity__perplexity_research`
- **Query:** What production machine learning and AI solutions are deployed in construction and engineering firms (general contractors, specialty contractors, engineering consultancies)? Focus on specific business problems with documented deployments and measured ROI: bid/no-bid decision models, win-probability, change-order prediction, schedule delay risk prediction, cost overrun prediction, RFI/submittal automation, safety incident prediction, equipment utilization, estimating/takeoff automation. For each, name the vendors or companies that deployed it, the data used, the ML technique, and the measured results with sources.
- **Timestamp (UTC):** 2026-10-09T20:17:00Z

````
**The strongest documented deployments are in bid scoring, schedule-risk forecasting, document review, safety-risk ranking, and automated takeoffs.** Published results range from forecast accuracy and staff hours saved to avoided-delay estimates; they are not all audited financial ROI. The table distinguishes predictive ML from AI-assisted optimization and conventional equipment analytics.

| Business problem | Vendor / deployed company | Data used | ML / AI technique | Measured results (source) |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Bid/no-bid and win-probability scoring | AnswerRocket **OptiCustomer** / **Western Specialty Contractors** | Western’s historical bids, evaluated alongside external economic indicators; scores integrated into Salesforce | Custom ML win/loss prediction; specific algorithm undisclosed | **Over 70% win/loss forecasting accuracy**. Western reports using scores to prioritize pursuits, but publishes no measured win-rate increase or dollar ROI.[web:108][web:106] |
| Schedule-delay risk prediction | **nPlan / Suffolk Construction**, Boston hospital extension | More than **11,000 Suffolk historical schedules** and the live project schedule | Deep-learning activity-duration and project-risk forecasts, updated as the schedule changed | Reported activity-level forecast accuracy rose **48% → 74%**. Suffolk acted on a flagged testing-and-balancing risk; the case study attributes **20 days and $1.25 million in avoided delay costs** to that intervention.[web:268] |
| Schedule-delay risk prediction | **Buildots / GTM Sud-Ouest TP GC and SOCOTRAP**, Blagnac construction project | Captured work progress compared with planned activity pace and pace needed to finish on time | AI progress analysis and **Delay Forecast**; model architecture undisclosed | Flagged slowing sprinkler and drywall work. The contractors **estimate** they avoided **2–3 days of delay for each of 10 subcontractors**, approximately **20 cumulative days**, through interventions using Buildots.[web:154] |
| Schedule recovery rather than probability prediction | **ALICE Technologies / Suffolk Construction**, life-sciences project | Project schedule, dependencies, procurement constraints, sequencing and trade-productivity assumptions | AI-assisted schedule interrogation and scenario optimization; not a published delay-probability model | Suffolk reports **42 days of delay recovered on critical milestones**: 22 from resequencing and 20 from targeted acceleration. This is a schedule-recovery result, not a disclosed ML forecast-accuracy or cash-ROI result.[web:200] |
| Cost-at-completion and overrun warning | **Doxel / Kaiser Permanente Viewridge Medical Office project team**; deployment was **owner-led**, with contractors working under integrated project delivery | Daily camera and laser scans, installed quantities, 3D design, budget line items and measured productivity | AI visual measurement of work in place, followed by earned-value and cost-at-completion forecasting | Doxel reports **96% final cost-at-completion prediction accuracy**, with **six times** the lead time of conventional forecasts; trade labor productivity rose **38%**, and the project finished **11% under budget**. The source does not isolate how much of the budget result Doxel caused.[web:144] |
| Progress and cost assurance | **Doxel / Layton Construction**, healthcare project | Captured site progress, installed-work quantities, planned work and billing | AI-powered progress measurement and production-rate analytics; forecasting algorithm undisclosed | Six site staff reduced manual progress tracking from **60 to 3 hours/week** (**95% less**); Doxel also reports **10% less overbilling**. These are measurement and billing outcomes, not a published cost-overrun prediction score.[web:132] |
| Change-order exposure and contract-risk detection—not prediction of future change orders | **Provision / EllisDon** | Contracts, base and supplementary conditions, clauses, revisions, comments and project-specific risk checklists | Automated document review, comparison and risk identification; model architecture undisclosed | Across **40 analyses**, EllisDon reports **2,221 risks identified**, **240+ hours saved** reconciling conditions, and **CAD $1.8 million/year in project savings**. Provision also reports **$100,000+ in potential claims avoided**. It does **not** report a model that predicts whether a future change order will occur.[web:122] |
| RFI preparation from design discrepancies | **Firmus AI-REVIEW / Flintco**, healthcare facility | Drawing sets, door schedules, floor plans and other design views | AI design-document review to find conflicts and scope gaps for human-organized RFIs; specific model undisclosed | Review reported to take **48 hours rather than 2–3 weeks**; Flintco said the resulting RFI preparation alone saved **days**. This is issue detection and RFI generation, not future-RFI prediction.[web:314] |
| Answers to field questions from project documents | **Trunk Tools TrunkText / Gilbane**, Baird Center expansion | **20,600+ project documents**, totaling 33.7 GB | AI document question-answering with project-source-backed answers | In a **37-working-day pilot**, staff asked **246 questions**, saving an estimated **20–40 minutes each**; the team validated **87% answer correctness**. The vendor calculates **6.5× ROI from labor alone** and **40× including potential rework prevention**, which is less certain than the observed query count.[web:227] |
| Document search and submittal review | **Trunk Tools / Torcon**, two active projects | Drawings, specifications, prior submittals, RFIs and other project documents | Source-backed AI Q&A plus AI-assisted submittal compliance review | In four months, approximately **1,100 queries saved 453 hours**; **64 submittal reviews saved another approximately 46 hours**. Torcon was piloting the tools on live projects.[web:228] |
| Submittal review | **Trunk Tools TrunkSubmittal / Cleveland Construction**, four projects | Submittals and project information in its Autodesk Forma environment | AI-assisted document search and compliance review; specific model undisclosed | Reported **790+ hours saved**, **$60,000+ in estimated labor-cost savings**, and reviews compressed from **days to hours** across four projects.[web:236] |
| Safety-incident risk prediction | **Newmetrix / JE Dunn** | 2016–2021 project-location, weather, staffing, image and video data | Predictive AI ranking projects by incident risk; algorithm undisclosed | A National Safety Council case study says the **seven highest-ranked projects each week accounted for 75% of recordable incidents**; the deployment prompted about **350 additional safety conversations**. It does not establish an incident-rate reduction attributable to the model.[web:237] |
| Prioritizing safety and quality issues | **Autodesk Construction IQ / BAM Ireland** | BIM 360 issues, observations, checklists, subcontractor assignments and project metadata | ML-based daily risk identification and prioritization; specific model undisclosed | BAM reported a **20% reduction in quality and safety issues** and **25% more staff time focused on high-risk issues**. Autodesk does not publish BAM’s comparison period or a separate incident-only result.[web:80] |
| Equipment utilization and avoidable rentals | **EquipmentShare T3 / Lindamood Demolition** | Equipment GPS location, run and idle hours, engine health and maintenance thresholds | **Utilization reporting and threshold rules; no ML technique documented for this result** | Lindamood reports **65% higher fleet utilization** and **25% lower rental costs** after acting on the reports. This is a useful measured equipment benchmark, **not evidence of an ML utilization model**.[web:104] |
| Equipment idle-time reduction | **Tenna / Boudreau Pipeline** | Asset locations, utilization and idle-time reports | **Telematics reporting; no ML technique documented for this result** | Reported **397 fewer idle hours** from August to September 2023 and **$15,000–$19,000/month in estimated savings**. It should not be counted as proven ML ROI.[web:96] |
| Automated estimating and takeoff | **Togal.AI / Coastal Construction**, Miami office | Construction plans and drawing revisions | Deep-learning detection, labeling and measurement of spaces, walls and objects | Coastal reports **14.5 hours saved per plan set** and approximately **$1 million in first-year time-value savings** in its earlier case study. A later case study reports **140–230 hours saved per project**, **50–65% less preconstruction effort**, and **$3.2 million in *estimated* 2025 annual savings**; it says other process changes also contributed.[web:184][web:181] |
| Automated specialty-contractor takeoff | **Beam AI / Southern Illinois Piping Contractors** | Mechanical/plumbing plans, specifications and drawing addenda | AI-assisted quantity takeoffs and document review; model architecture undisclosed | Takeoff time fell by approximately **50%**, saving **6–8 hours per project**; bids went out **2–3 days earlier**. The contractor/vendor case study attributes **$250,000 in additional revenue**, **not profit**, to increased bidding capacity.[web:182] |

**Evidence boundary:** I found no named, measured production case establishing *future change-order occurrence prediction* or **ML-driven equipment-utilization ROI**; the closest documented deployments above detect contract risks or use telematics reports. AECOM has published a promising ML cost-estimating comparison—less than 4% error versus 8% for manual estimates—but describes it as a **trial**, not a confirmed production deployment.[web:122][web:104][web:96][web:259]
````
