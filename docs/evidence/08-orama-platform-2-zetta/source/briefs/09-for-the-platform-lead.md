# 09 — For the platform / AI lead

**Logline:** The bottleneck is structural — every request for intelligence routes through one platform team. Orama Zetta takes that team out of the middle without giving up control.

**For:** the platform/AI lead at a mid-to-large company (the Charles persona). High technical comfort; CEO breathing down their neck; everything funnels through them.

---

Every team wants intelligence over the company's data, and the only way they get it is by waiting on the platform team. The pipeline already exists — maybe a hand-built automation wired to a sidecar and a database that needs constant patching — and it works, but it's bespoke, and it doesn't scale past one team's hours. The business has called the platform team slow for two years. The real diagnosis is structural, not a skill gap: everything routes through one team — a queue's worth of demand funneled through a single point.

Orama Zetta lets you hand your developers a thing they run themselves. Import the workflow you already built; it re-architects it correctly (a real predictive model where you had an LLM guessing), and packages it as a build your team downloads, points at the data, and iterates on — without you in the loop for every request. You stay in control of the architecture and the governance; you stop being the manual step.

And it deploys where you live — inside your firewall, behind your identity provider, your data never leaving. The thing you'd have spent a quarter productionizing becomes something your devs are using this sprint.

**Talking points**
- The bottleneck is structural (a queue through one person), not a skill gap — adding people barely helps.
- Hand devs a downloadable build they run unaided; you own architecture + governance, not every request.
- Re-architects the existing hand-built automation + sidecar — a correct predictive model instead of an LLM guess.
- Deploys in your firewall, behind your IdP; data never leaves.

**Lines worth saying**
- "The bottleneck isn't speed — it's structure: every request routes through one team."
- "Hand your devs the thing. Keep the control. Lose the bottleneck."

**Show (visual beats)**
- The lead as the single chokepoint → the chokepoint dissolving as devs self-serve.
- Their hand-built automation + sidecar diagram → a clean, deployed Orama Zetta graph.
