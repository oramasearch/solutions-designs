**Practical read:** For a buyer comparing controls software, ask vendors to specify the baseline and measurement period, whether savings mean HVAC kWh or total building energy, and whether the result is independently verified. Distinguish subscription pricing from shared-savings contracts: the available BrainBox sources support monthly SaaS pricing, but do not establish shared savings as its standard model. [1][4] For incentives, don’t assume software qualifies just because efficiency hardware does; confirm eligibility with the local utility or program administrator. [8]

The search results available for this answer substantiate only BrainBox AI and Montana Technologies/AirJoule in detail; the other vendors’ metrics and commercial terms need vendor-specific verification.
