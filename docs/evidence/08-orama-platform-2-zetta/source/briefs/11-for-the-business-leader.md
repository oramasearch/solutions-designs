# 11 — For the business leader / analyst

**Logline:** You shouldn't have to file a ticket and wait two weeks to learn what your own data already knows. Ask, and get a grounded answer — within what you're allowed to see.

**For:** executives, BizOps, analysts (the Sam persona). No engineering background; lives in reports.

---

Right now, getting an answer that isn't already on a dashboard means asking someone technical and waiting — days, weeks, sometimes never. So you make decisions on stale reports, or on instinct, while the answer sits in data nobody had time to query. And you don't want to *build* a dashboard; you want to know what to focus on today.

With Orama Zetta, the technical team sets up the platform once, and then you ask in plain language — "which accounts are most likely to churn this quarter, and why" — and get a grounded answer, with its sources shown, scoped to exactly the data your role is allowed to touch. You can ask it to run a check every Monday and ping you in Slack. No ticket, no wait, no dashboard to maintain.

The trust comes from the platform showing its work: where the number came from, how fresh it is, how confident it is, and what it couldn't see. So it's not a black-box oracle — it's an answer you can stand behind in a meeting.

**Talking points**
- No ticket, no two-week wait — a plain-language question returns a grounded answer.
- Scoped to your access — you only see what you're allowed to.
- "What should I focus on today" — not another dashboard to maintain.
- Trust through transparency: sources, freshness, confidence, and what's missing are shown.

**Lines worth saying**
- "Your data already knows. You shouldn't have to wait two weeks to hear it."
- "Not a dashboard to build. An answer you can take into the meeting."

**Show (visual beats)**
- A typed question → a clean answer with citations + a freshness/confidence badge.
- A "every Monday, 8am, to #revenue" schedule toggle.
