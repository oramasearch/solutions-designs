# 03 — Three disciplines, one workflow

**Logline:** Building real intelligence takes a platform engineer, a data engineer, and a data scientist. Orama Zetta delivers what all three would build — as a single workflow.

---

Turning a question into a working system normally needs three different skill sets. A **data engineer** connects the sources, cleans the mess, and shapes the data. A **data scientist** builds models and pipelines that actually predict something — not an LLM guessing, but real trained models with measured accuracy. A **platform engineer** deploys it somewhere safe and keeps it running. Most teams are short on all three, and the handoffs between them are where months disappear.

Orama Zetta collapses the three into one workflow. The customer brings the intent or the artifact; the platform does the data engineering (connect, clean, profile), the data science (architect and train the right models and pipelines, check they're correct), and the platform engineering (deploy the database and the workflow, govern it, run it). No handoffs, no waiting in three different queues.

The standard of the output is the point: this is not advice or a copilot suggesting code that still has to be assembled. It's the finished, deployed, running system — the production-grade infrastructure those three specialists would build, correct by construction and yours to inspect and adjust. Production-grade, not demoware.

**Talking points**
- Three scarce disciplines — platform engineer, data engineer, data scientist — in one workflow.
- The production-grade infrastructure three specialists would build, delivered as a single flow.
- Not a copilot that hands you code; the finished, deployed, running system.
- Real models and pipelines with measured accuracy, not an LLM guessing over your data.
- Removes the handoffs where months are lost.
