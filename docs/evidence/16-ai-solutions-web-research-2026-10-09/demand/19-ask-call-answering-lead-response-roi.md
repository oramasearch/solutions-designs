**Dental:** The results available here do not establish a named 2025–2026 dental practice with auditable before/after missed-call, booking, or recovered-revenue figures for Weave, Dentina, Arini, or Mia Labs. Their inclusion in the question is not enough to substantiate a numeric case study.

**Auto dealerships:** No named 2025–2026 dealership case study with before/after AI-answering revenue or booking figures is substantiated in the available results. The benchmark data below concerns dealership lead-response behavior, not AI-attributed revenue.

## Benchmarks and caveats

- **Home-service missed calls:** The estimates vary substantially by source and method. One benchmark puts the rate at roughly **27%**; another reports **14%** for home services, while a separate 2026 compilation gives a broad **27%–62%** range.[5][7][8] Treat any single figure as a directional benchmark, not a universal rate.
- **Dental and auto response time:** A 2026 compilation reports a **5-hour-20-minute median** response for dental leads. For auto inquiries, it reports that **61% of dealers respond within 15 minutes**, **19% take more than an hour**, and a mystery-shop study found an average first response of over **9 hours**.[8]
- **Home-service lead response:** The same compilation reports a **3-hour-40-minute median** for general home services and **3 hours 12 minutes** for HVAC; its recommended targets are much faster.[8]
- **Speed matters, but it is not a revenue forecast:** The often-cited lead-response study found that, compared with a 30-minute response, contacting a lead within 5 minutes was associated with **21× higher odds of qualification**. That is an odds comparison from an older study—not a 21× increase in bookings or revenue.[7]

**Bottom line:** Public case studies support meaningful gains in captured calls and booking rates for some home-service operators, including a reported **70%→90%** booking-rate improvement and **20% year-over-year revenue growth** in one Avoca customer example.[4] The evidence provided does **not** support similarly specific, named 2025–2026 revenue-recovery claims for dental practices or auto dealerships.
